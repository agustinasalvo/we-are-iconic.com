About the Gathering

01

Three days in Barcelona to reconnect, exchange ideas, learn from one another and celebrate the power of a global community.

This is not simply an itinerary.

It is a shared experience designed around conversation, discovery, inspiration and connection.

WE ARE ICONIC — Barcelona brings together an intimate circle of international women for three days shaped by dialogue, culture, craft and celebration — set against a city built on light, geometry and imagination.
